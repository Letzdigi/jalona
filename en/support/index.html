Support

A question or a problem? Write to t.diederich@bplus.lu. Mention the device (iPhone or Windows) and the version (in the app, at the bottom of the account screen).

Getting access

Your office’s management adds you in Jalona Office (Paramètres → Accès) and gives you a provisional password. At your first sign-in Jalona Construct asks you to choose your own (at least 10 characters); it then works in Jalona Construct and in Jalona Office.

Forgotten password

Ask your office’s management to send you a reset link. The link in the e-mail opens the new password page, where you choose a new one. The link works once; if it has expired, ask for another.

Changing your password

If you know it: in the app, office account screen → Changer mon mot de passe.

Updates

  • iPhone: installed from TestFlight or the App Store, the app updates itself. Jalona Construct’s content can also update from inside the app: Chantier → Rechercher une mise à jour.
  • Windows: Jalona Construct offers the new version when it starts.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later; PC with Windows 10 or 11.

Don’t delete the app to “start over”

Deleting Jalona Construct from the iPhone also deletes anything not yet synced. If something goes wrong, contact support first; meanwhile Sauvegarder les projets keeps a copy.
