Jalona Construct

An architecture office’s building sites in one app: site meetings, open points, reports, photos, plans and schedule, on Windows and iPhone.

SupportAccess, password, updates, contact. PrivacyWhat data, where, for how long.

Who it is for

Jalona Construct is used by offices that manage their projects in Jalona Office. There is no public sign-up: the office’s management creates the accounts and grants access to each site.

On site

  • Record a point with a photo, assign it to a contractor, follow it until it is closed.
  • Prepare the meeting and issue the report as a PDF.
  • View PDF plans and the schedule, even offline.
  • Everything syncs with the office as soon as the connection is back.
