Privacy policy

Updated 26 October 2026

Jalona Construct lets an architecture office follow its building sites. This page says what data the app handles, where it is kept and what you can ask for.

Who is responsible

Jalona Construct is published by Tom Diederich (t.diederich@bplus.lu). A site’s data belongs to the office that manages it: the office decides who has access and how long it is kept. Jalona Construct is the tool that processes it on the office’s behalf.

Data handled

  • Your account: e-mail address and password. The password is stored hashed by the sign-in service; nobody can read it.
  • Site content: name and addresses of the site, contractors (companies, names, e-mails, phone numbers), meetings, points, reports, schedule, PDF plans and photos.
  • On the device: a copy of the sites to work offline, and your sign-in.

The camera and photo library are used only when you take or pick a photo for a point.

Where it is kept

In the office’s Supabase project, hosted in Ireland (European Union). App updates are downloaded from GitHub. No other company receives your data.

What Jalona Construct does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data.

Retention

As long as the office keeps the site. When a project is archived in Jalona Office, its content leaves the server. Copies on a device stay until the project or the app is deleted there.

Deleting your account

Ask your office’s management, or write to t.diederich@bplus.lu. The account is deleted within 30 days; what you wrote in the sites stays with the office, which is responsible for it.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction, erasure or restriction by writing to the address above, and complain to Luxembourg’s data protection authority, the CNPD (cnpd.public.lu).
